# Maintainers

## Collaborators

Collaborators are listed here as they join.

## What collaborators do

Collaborators have write access. They review pull requests, triage issues and labels, and merge pull requests once they are approved.

- Every change goes through a pull request. Nobody pushes to `main` directly.
- The code owner stays [@YoanWai](https://github.com/YoanWai) (see [CODEOWNERS](.github/CODEOWNERS)), so every merge needs his approval.
- Tags and releases are cut by the lead maintainer only.
- Changes to workflows, release configuration, or secrets need a pull request approved by the lead maintainer.
- Two-factor authentication is required on your GitHub account. Use an authenticator app or a security key, not SMS.

## Trial period

A new collaborator starts with a 90-day trial. At the end of it, the lead maintainer and the collaborator decide together whether to continue, and whether to extend code ownership to the areas the collaborator works on.

## Stepping back

Anyone can step back at any time. After six months without activity, a collaborator moves to the Emeritus list below, with thanks, and their access is removed. Ask to come back at any time.
